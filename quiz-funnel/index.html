Questions

Before you start

Do I have to buy anything?

No. The check and your report are free. If you'd like our help afterward, we'll show you how it works, and you can decide from there.

Will this tell me which plan to buy?

No. A plan choice depends on details a quiz can't see, like your exact doctors and medications. Your report shows which areas need a closer look before you decide.

Will an insurance agent call me?

No. We're not an insurance agent, we don't sell insurance, and we're not paid by insurance companies. If anyone reaches out, it's Emily's own team.

I'm already on Medicare. Is this still useful?

Yes. You'll get a report built for people already on Medicare, including what to watch for at each year's open enrollment.
