Questions

Before you get started

Are you an insurance agent or broker?

No. We're not an insurance company, an agent, a broker, or the government. We don't sell any plans and we don't get paid anything by insurance companies. Clients hire us directly, the same way they'd hire an accountant.

What does the Medicare Enrollment Concierge cost?

It's a one-time service at $1,497, or two payments of $749. If you're married, your spouse can join for $700, since couples oftentimes end up on different plans with different doctors and medications.

Is it a yearly fee?

No. The Concierge happens once, to get you on Medicare correctly the first time. Your first money-saving annual review is included. After that, annual reviews are optional.

When should I start?

Ideally 6 to 12 months before you want Medicare to begin. Starting early gives your coach time to catch red flags, like a doctor who doesn't take Medicare. If you're closer than that, we move faster and cover what must happen before you finalize anything.

I'm working past 65 on an employer plan. Is this for me?

Yes. Your coach compares your employer plan side by side with Medicare. If you're better off staying on your employer plan for now, your Concierge still covers you for whenever you do join Medicare.

Can you fill out my supplement application for me?

That's the one thing we can't do, because the application with an insurance company has to go through an agent. That's actually the proof that we're not paid by them. We help with every other step.
