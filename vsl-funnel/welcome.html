Three things to gather before your first call

  1. Your doctor listEvery doctor you see now or might see in the next year or two. Call each one and ask: what type of Medicare do you accept, and are there any rules I should know about, like a limit on Medicare patients?
  2. Your medications and pharmacyNames, doses and how often you take them, plus the pharmacy you use.
  3. Any employer or retiree plan detailsIf you or your spouse have coverage past 65, have the plan summary handy so your coach can compare it side by side with Medicare.

Joining as a couple? Keep your lists separate. Each of you gets your own research.
